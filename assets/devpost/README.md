## Final image-generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Devpost gallery hero background for a hackathon mixed-reality project. Create a polished, photorealistic-but-clearly-conceptual first-person mixed-reality view inside a modern university classroom or hackathon lab. The viewer sees two natural tracked hands, with one hand performing a subtle index-finger-to-thumb pinch toward a real chair in the foreground. A laptop sits on a desk farther away. Several elegant translucent spatial annotation cards are visibly attached to the real objects by thin leader lines, with accurate perspective and depth; leave the cards blank because exact typography will be added later. Include a subtle central aiming ray/reticle. The image must immediately communicate that an AI system labels physical objects in 3D space. Use a cinematic 16:9 composition, natural daylight, warm neutrals, restrained cyan and amber accents, and negative space for title/disclosure. No logos, brand marks, legible text, watermark, headset frame, cyberpunk styling, or sci-fi HUD overload. Keep the room and hands visually plausible.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied into the repository and combined with deterministic SVG typography. This is concept art, not evidence of Quest behavior. The labels use two supported demo categories, but they are illustrative rather than recognition results.
